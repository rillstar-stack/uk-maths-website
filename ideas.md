# Chosen Direction — Quiet Scholarly House

## Design Movement

**Editorial British scholasticism** with the warmth of independent bookshops and the calm discipline of a well-kept tutor's study. This is a personal practice, not an institutional platform: the design must feel informed, steady, and human.

## Core Principles

1. **Personal authority, never corporate scale.** Editorial spacing, a direct first-person voice, and a portrait placeholder make the site feel like Mrs. James's own home online.
2. **Quiet confidence over visual noise.** Cream ground, deep ink, muted sage, and one warm ochre accent deliver reassurance rather than overstimulation.
3. **Learning made legible.** Tables, resource cards, booking guidance, and enquiry controls are clear, well labelled, and easy to scan on a phone.
4. **Progress is a practice.** Subtle mathematical rule-lines, considered motion, and incremental motifs express the idea that confidence grows through steady work.

## Color Philosophy

The palette uses **ink navy** as the trusted, academic anchor; **paper cream** to give content breathing room; **sage green** as a quiet signal of growth; and **ochre gold** for decisive next steps. Navy will establish competence, cream will make the experience approachable, and gold will be reserved for meaningful invitations such as booking a consultation.

## Layout Paradigm

The page follows an **editorial reading path** rather than a centred card grid. It opens with a split, off-centre hero; then moves through alternating wide-and-narrow sections, a marked timetable, a bordered booking room, a horizontal resource shelf, and a considered closing enquiry panel. At larger widths, select sections use a vertical margin rail for labels and mathematical fragments.

## Signature Elements

1. A fine **coordinate-paper / pencil-rule texture** used sparingly in backgrounds and dividers.
2. A small, circular **compass-and-spark mark** that appears in the header, list labels, and favicon.
3. **Ocher index tabs** and short ruled underlines that make key calls to action feel like highlighted study notes.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should give quiet confirmation. Links lift by a pixel, buttons press inward, resource cards reveal a precise action label, and navigation scrolls purposefully to the relevant section. There are no distracting celebratory effects, animated counters, or endlessly moving elements.

## Animation

Use a 160–220ms custom ease-out for hover and focus transitions. On first load, hero copy and visual elements can rise 10px with a short, staggered opacity transition. Section content may fade in once when it enters the viewport; all non-essential motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The booking and enquiry status messaging should appear instantly enough to feel dependable.

## Typography System

Use **Cormorant Garamond** for display headlines and key editorial pull quotes, paired with **DM Sans** for body copy, form labels, tables, and actions. Headlines are generous but unforced; small labels use a tracked uppercase DM Sans treatment, and body copy stays at comfortable 16–18px equivalent sizes for parents reading on mobile.

## Brand Essence

**Mrs. Liping James Maths Tuition is patient, expert one-to-one and small-group maths support for UK families who want confidence as well as results.**

**Personality:** Reassuring, exacting, encouraging.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are plain-spoken and quietly optimistic. Calls to action feel invitational and specific; microcopy makes the next step feel easy and clearly bounded.

Example headline: “Maths confidence grows one clear step at a time.”

Example CTA: “Arrange a free 15-minute conversation.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark pairs a refined serif name with a small **compass-and-spark** symbol: a single open arc, a fine vertical line, and a four-point glint suggest direction, precision, and the moment a concept becomes clear. The logo mark must work alone in the favicon and header; the full wordmark is composed in the interface rather than using generated text.

## Signature Brand Color

**Margin Ochre — #C78A2C.** A warm, restrained golden brown that recalls a carefully highlighted margin note and is used only to guide attention.

## Style Decisions

- Public-facing copy will remain personal and final in tone; operational setup language belongs in the implementation handoff rather than in the page interface.
- Margin Ochre remains a margin-note accent for primary invitations, numerals, small markers and fine highlights, while cream, navy and sage carry the larger spatial atmosphere.
- Coordinate-paper textures, pencil rules, margin labels and study-note surfaces recur across major sections so the Quiet Scholarly House motif is consistently recognisable.
